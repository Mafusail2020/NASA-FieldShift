source: General agronomy summary written for Field Shift; thresholds are the project's estimates unless stated.
status: estimate

# Water stress
Crops stress when the root zone holds less than about half of its readily available water. In the steppe south, rainfall is below reference evapotranspiration (ETo) for most of the year, so yield depends on stored winter moisture and spring-early summer rain. A year is "dry" in the app when annual rain is far below the 1991-2025 mean of the cell.

# Heat
Heat damages crops mostly at flowering and grain fill. The app counts days over 32 and 35 C in the grid layers and over crop-specific thresholds in rotations. A trend that is not significant (p at or above 0.05) should not be presented as a real change.

# Frost
Late spring frost hurts early-sown and emerged crops; early autumn frost hurts late crops. The app reports the day of year of the last spring and first autumn frost per cell at 0, -2 and -4 C. Winter frost damage to winter crops depends on snow cover; the app uses a temperature-based snow estimate.

# Disease weather (context only)
Leaf diseases of cereals (rusts, septoria, powdery mildew) and Fusarium head blight are favoured by warm humid spells at key stages, especially rain around flowering. Late blight type epidemics need prolonged leaf wetness. The app does not model disease; weather history can show whether past seasons were humid at a stage, but diagnosis needs a person who sees the plants.

# Season twins
A season twin compares the weather since sowing with the same window in earlier years and lists the three closest. It is a way to see how similar seasons ended, not a forecast; three matches out of 35 years is a small sample.

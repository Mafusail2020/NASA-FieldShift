# Sugar beet
Sown early April, harvested September-October. Highest water demand of the common crops; yield follows summer rain and irrigation closely. Return no sooner than 4-5 years because of nematodes and Cercospora. Leaves the field relatively early, and the soil is loose, but residues of tops are rich in nitrogen.

# Black fallow
A field kept free of crops and weeds for one season to store soil water and nitrate before winter wheat. In dry southern steppe it can raise wheat yield, but it leaves the soil bare for months (erosion, loss of humus through mineralisation) and earns nothing that year. The app counts bare-soil months and the soil water at sowing for this reason. Occupied fallow (fast green manure or early crop) is often a better trade-off where it is allowed.
